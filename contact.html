Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our programs and services

What are the eligibility requirements?

Eligibility requirements vary by program. Generally, for undergraduate programs, you need a high school diploma or equivalent. For postgraduate programs, a bachelor's degree is required. Specific requirements are available on the university websites.

How long do programs take to complete?

Undergraduate programs typically take 3 years, while postgraduate programs take 2 years. Professional courses like B.Ed can vary from 2-4 years. You can check specific durations in our Programs page.

What is the cost of the programs?

Program fees vary depending on the university, program, and duration. We offer affordable fees with flexible payment options. Contact us for detailed fee information for your chosen program.

How does distance education work?

Distance education involves self-paced learning through study materials, online resources, and digital platforms. You attend periodic on-campus sessions for exams and practical training. This flexibility allows you to balance education with work and family commitments.

Are distance education degrees recognized?

Yes! Our partner universities are government-recognized and their degrees are valued equally to regular degrees. All our universities are NAAC accredited, ensuring quality and recognition.

How do I apply for admission?

You can start by filling an inquiry form on our website or contact us directly. We will guide you through the application process, help you choose the right program, and assist with admission to your chosen university.

What support services are available?

We provide comprehensive support including academic guidance, career counseling, library access, online resources, and regular communication with university. Our team is available to help throughout your academic journey.

Can I transfer credits from other universities?

Credit transfer policies vary by university and program. Some universities allow transfers of completed coursework from accredited institutions. Contact us to discuss your specific situation.
